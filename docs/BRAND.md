# TidyMemo — Brand direction

## Product idea

TidyMemo is a local desktop workspace for organizing and maintaining personal
photo and video memories.

It is not positioned as a photo editor, a cloud gallery, or a generic file
manager. Its role is to help people keep a media collection understandable,
orderly, and reasonably sized over time.

## Core promise

> Organize, understand, and lighten your photo and video memories — locally.

French signature:

> Vos souvenirs, enfin bien rangés.

## Personality

- Reassuring rather than technical
- Clear rather than feature-heavy
- Warm rather than nostalgic
- Careful rather than automated at all costs
- Practical rather than decorative

User-facing language should favor **photos**, **videos**, **souvenirs**,
**collection**, and **fichiers**. Terms such as **EXIF**, **codec**, and
**transcoding** should appear only where they help users make a decision.

## Visual territory

The identity should combine two ideas:

1. **Order** — alignment, sequence, folders, a tidy stack, or a check mark.
2. **Memory** — a photograph, a frame, a play symbol, or a moment in time.

The logo must remain readable as a 16–32 px desktop icon. Avoid camera-aperture
logos, cloud symbols, and literal brooms: they are either overused or suggest the
wrong product category.

## Logo directions

### 1. The tidy stack

Three slightly offset media cards become progressively aligned. The front card
contains a small play triangle or image horizon, allowing the same symbol to
represent photos and videos. A rounded silhouette makes it reassuring rather
than administrative.

Why it fits: it communicates a collection being put in order without suggesting
that TidyMemo stores the collection itself.

### 2. The TM frame

A monogram where the vertical stroke of `T` divides a rounded photo frame and the
two arches of `M` suggest mountains or a timeline. This direction is compact and
works well for an application icon, but requires careful drawing to avoid looking
like a generic corporate monogram.

### 3. The checked memory

A rounded media tile whose lower edge turns into a subtle check mark. A small sun
or play symbol identifies it as media. This emphasizes safe completion and the
preview-before-action behavior of the product.

### 4. The ordered timeline

Three dots representing moments move along a line and settle into evenly spaced
positions inside a rounded frame. This direction expresses chronology and works
particularly well in motion for the website or splash screen.

### 5. The memory tray — selected

A warm archive tray holds two large cards: a sage photo card and an orange video
card. The cards rise above the tray so both media types remain identifiable at a
glance. This direction has been selected as the production application icon.

Why it fits: the tray conveys care and organization without implying deletion,
while the two cards give photos and videos equal importance.

## Recommended art direction

Use **the memory tray** as the primary icon. The dark green is the main color,
with warm ivory, sage, and orange for the three foreground elements. The symbol
must also have a successful one-color version for small toolbar contexts.

Suggested construction:

- Rounded-square application icon
- Dark forest-green background
- Warm ivory archive tray
- Sage photo card with a minimal landscape
- Orange video card with a light play triangle
- No text inside the application icon
- `TidyMemo` wordmark beside the symbol in larger contexts

## Logo acceptance checklist

- Recognizable at 16, 24, 32, 64, and 256 px
- Understandable without the wordmark
- Distinct in color and monochrome
- Does not imply cloud storage or photo editing
- Represents both still images and video
- Works on light and dark operating-system themes
- Can be exported cleanly as SVG, PNG, and ICO
